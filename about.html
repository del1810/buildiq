/ ABOUT BUILDIQ 2027

Building the Future of Intelligent Spaces

The evolution of buildings is no longer limited to architecture, construction and physical infrastructure. Intelligent technologies are becoming integral to modern homes, offices, commercial developments, hospitality facilities, healthcare establishments, industrial campuses and institutional buildings.

Smart building solutions are enabling greater operational efficiency, enhanced security, improved occupant comfort, intelligent energy utilisation and centralised management of building systems.

BUILDIQ is conceived as an industry-focused exhibition that brings these technologies and their stakeholders together on a common platform. The exhibition will showcase a comprehensive range of smart home, building automation, intelligent electrical, lighting, security, audio-visual, networking, energy management and integrated building solutions.

BUILDIQ aims to bridge the gap between technology manufacturers and the professionals responsible for specifying, designing, integrating, developing, procuring and managing modern buildings. By bringing together technology brands, solution providers, channel partners and project decision-makers, BUILDIQ seeks to create a commercially relevant environment for product discovery, industry collaboration and business opportunities.

/ MARKET OPPORTUNITY

The Growing Demand for Intelligent Buildings in India

India's building and infrastructure landscape is undergoing a significant transformation. Rapid urbanisation, expanding commercial developments, modern residential projects, digital infrastructure and increasing technology adoption are creating new opportunities for smart building solutions.

The growing focus on energy efficiency, operational optimisation, occupant comfort, security and connected building management is encouraging developers and building owners to adopt intelligent technologies.

Smart home automation, intelligent lighting, integrated security, building management systems, IoT-enabled devices and energy monitoring solutions are increasingly relevant across residential and commercial applications.

BUILDIQ aims to provide a focused business platform for companies seeking to participate in these evolving market opportunities.
